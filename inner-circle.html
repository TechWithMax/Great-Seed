Awakening does not end after one consultation. The Inner Circle is where the work continues, in community.

Why a circle

No one blooms alone.

The Inner Circle is our standing fellowship — a smaller, closer community for those ready to walk the path of awakening together rather than in isolation. Members gather weekly for prayer and teaching, receive priority access to consultations, and share in seasonal retreats.

It is not a program to complete. It is a table you stay seated at.
