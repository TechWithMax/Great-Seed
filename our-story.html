Our Story

About the Founder.

A great seed does not remain buried. It dies to itself, rises up, and becomes a tree that provides shelter, fruit, and shade for many. This is the story of Isaac Anyimah—a seed that resisted the soil for a season, but when finally planted, grew to become a blessing to his generation.

Great Seed Herbal Ministries

Rooted in purpose. Growing in service.

How it began

The Salt of Nzema.

Isaac Anyimah was born and raised in the rich cultural lands of Nzema—a place that shaped his roots and became the soil where his divine purpose would one day take root. From his earliest years, Isaac was known for his quiet spirit and a depth of wisdom that seemed beyond his age.

At the tender age of 12, while most children were still discovering the world around them, Isaac experienced a divine encounter that would forever mark his life. He was called into ministry—a clear invitation to serve God and His people. But the weight of such a calling felt too heavy for a young boy, and in the years that followed, the allure of the world began to pull at his heart.

It was in the Netherlands, far from the familiar shores of Nzema, that the divine calling returned—not in a whisper, but through vivid dreams. Isaac saw himself standing before multitudes, preaching a message of hope and liberation. He saw people bound by sin, sickness, and sorrow, and saw himself being used as an instrument of their freedom.

Today, Great Seed has returned to his roots and stands as the Salt of Nzema—a preserving, healing, and flavouring presence in his beloved homeland. Just as salt preserves and adds flavour to what it touches, his ministry brings preservation to crumbling lives, flavour to weary souls, and hope to the hopeless.

“A seed planted in purpose can become shelter, fruit, and shade for many.”

Milestones

Seasons of growth.

Every season has shaped the ministry, deepened its purpose, and prepared the way for what comes next.

1996

The Divine Call

The voice of God unmistakably marks him for a special purpose, though the weight of the calling feels too great for a young boy to bear. This moment becomes the seed that would lie dormant, yet never die, for years to come.

2001–2006

Establishing the Work

Great Seed becomes affectionately known as the “Salt of Nzema”— a preserving and healing presence in his community.

2016

The Tree Bears Fruit

Documented miracles and deliverances become a hallmark of the ministry. Souls are saved, bodies healed, and families restored across Nzema and beyond.

Today

A Growing Community

Great Seed now walks alongside hundreds of souls through consultation, ministry, and Inner Circle fellowship. Discipleship and leadership training programmes are helping raise the next generation of ministers.
