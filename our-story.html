Our Story

Planted in scarcity. Grown in faith.

The story of how a kitchen table apothecary became a ministry for the whole self.

How it began

A remedy for the body was never enough.

Great Seed Herbal Ministry began in a small kitchen, brewing teas for neighbors who could not afford a clinic visit. What started as simple remedies quickly became something larger — people did not just need their bodies tended to, they needed somewhere to bring their grief, their doubt, their exhaustion.

We began sitting with people the way our grandmothers once did: a cup of something warm, an open Bible, and enough silence for the truth to surface. That combination — herb and word, root and prayer — became the ministry you see today.

Milestones

Seasons of growth.

2016

The first tea table

Founder Mother Adobea begins offering herbal remedies and prayer from her home in the evenings, after her regular work hours.

2019

The ministry is formalized

Great Seed becomes an official ministry, welcoming its first circle of twelve members committed to weekly gathering and study.

2022

A home of our own

The ministry moves into its first dedicated space — a garden, a consultation room, and a hall for gathering.

Today

A growing community

Great Seed now walks alongside hundreds of souls through consultation, ministry, and the Inner Circle fellowship.

What we hold to

Our values.

  • Rooted honesty
