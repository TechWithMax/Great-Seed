What to expect.

Your first consultation lasts about 45 minutes, in person at our ministry home or by video call for those further away. We'll ask about your physical health, your spiritual season, and what brought you to us — there is no wrong answer.

From there, we'll recommend a path: a herbal plan, a counseling rhythm, or both. Nothing is prescribed without your full understanding of what it involves.
